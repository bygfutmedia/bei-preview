10 member questions
What do I get as a BEI member?+
As a member, you gain access to networking opportunities, business support, training, events, and a community of like-minded entrepreneurs.
How do I find and register for events?+
You can view upcoming workshops, webinars, and networking events on our Events page. Registration links will be provided for each event.
How can I promote my business on BEI?+
You can update your profile, share your products or services, and take part in our member spotlights to increase visibility.
Does BEI offer funding opportunities?+
We share information on grants, loans, and investment opportunities relevant to Black entrepreneurs. Keep an eye on our Funding section for updates.
How can I connect with other members?+
Use our member directory and discussion spaces to reach out, collaborate, and build partnerships with other entrepreneurs.
What training and mentorship is available?+
We offer training programs, mentorship opportunities, and expert-led workshops to help you grow your business.
How do I update my profile or share content?+
Log in to your account, go to your profile, and update your information. You can also share updates, resources, or articles with the community.
Where can I ask questions or get advice?+
Our community forum is the best place to ask for support, share your experiences, and connect with other members.
How does BEI celebrate member achievements?+
We highlight member success stories through newsletters, social media, and spotlights on the platform.
How can I share feedback with BEI?+
We welcome your ideas! You can send feedback directly through the platform or reach out to the BEI team anytime.
