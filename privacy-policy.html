Black Entrepreneurs International (“BEI,” “we,” “our,” “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website, membership platform, business directory, and newsletter.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information: Name, email, phone number, business details, and payment information when you register for membership, directory listing, or newsletter.

Usage Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and cookies.

Business Directory Data: Information you choose to publish in your directory profile (e.g. company name, services, contact info).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide membership benefits and process subscriptions.
  • Publish and maintain business directory listings.
  • Send newsletters, updates, and event invitations.
  • Improve website functionality and user experience.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing of Information

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • Information may be shared with trusted third-party service providers (e.g., payment processors, email marketing platforms) solely for operational purposes.
  • Business directory profiles may be publicly visible if you choose to be listed.

4. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

5. Data Retention & Security

We retain your personal data as long as your account is active or as required by law. We take reasonable measures to protect your data but cannot guarantee 100% security.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, update, or delete your personal information.
  • Unsubscribe from newsletters at any time.
  • Request removal of your business directory listing.
