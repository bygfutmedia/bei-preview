Our purpose

A Community Interest Company, reinvesting for community benefit.

Black Entrepreneurs International CIC (BEI) is a purpose-driven collective dedicated to building economic wealth using tangible, measurable outcomes. As a Community Interest Company (CIC), we reinvest profits for community benefit. Therefore, our activities always prioritize social impact over private gain.

Our Team

The people behind BEI

Black Entrepreneurs International CIC (BEI) was founded by two dedicated innovators committed to empowering and elevating Black-owned businesses across the UK and beyond.

With a shared vision for economic inclusion, sustainable growth, and community collaboration, the founders established BEI as a platform where entrepreneurs can access opportunities, build visibility and connect with new markets.

Their combined expertise and passion continue to shape BEI's mission to champion excellence, support enterprise, and create meaningful impact for every member.
